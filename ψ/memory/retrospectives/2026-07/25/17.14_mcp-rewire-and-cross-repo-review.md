## Lessons Learned

- When two parties disagree on a number, don't pick a side — reproduce independently and reconcile the difference (43 = 16 + 27; 20ms vs 5.8ms/scan). The reconciliation itself is the finding.
- After deleting an assertion/rule for having no effect, verify its replacement has an effect. "No-op check" is a shape that recurs; removing one instance doesn't inoculate the next line.
- A `setTimeout`/`Promise.race` timeout cannot detect a synchronously-blocked event loop — the timer is on the same loop. Use an OS-level timeout (separate process, `curl -m`, `timeout(1)`) to catch hangs.
- Before contradicting a claim about a file, `git status` it — a "wrong" implementation may be a half-written fix in the working tree.
- A recurring-interval loop must be paced to the task's real cycle time; an interval shorter than one iteration's latency produces noise, not progress. Push back on the mismatch early, don't absorb it.
